About this project

Why I’m building Aerospace Atlas.

I work in aerospace recruitment, and one thing I can help with is seeing the employment market beyond a single company or country.

What I can contribute

Engineers know their technical disciplines better than I do. My focus here is the part I work with: employers, locations, compensation, hiring conversations and the questions that come up when people consider a career move.

How I want this to work

Every published figure should have a source and a date. If I cannot establish a reliable number, I would rather leave it out. The tools are free, the notes are educational, and the directory will grow as the evidence improves.
