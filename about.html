A little about me

I'm Kevin Sunil, an aerospace headhunter based in Bristol, England.

I work at Piper Maddox, specialising in avionics, flight controls and simulation across the European aerospace, space, defence and autonomy markets.

Connect on LinkedIn ↗

What I do

My job involves speaking to engineers, founders, technical directors and hiring managers, from early-stage start-ups to established aerospace manufacturers. I get to see how different companies hire, what skills they look for and how engineers move between countries and industries.

Why I built Aerospace Atlas

Something I've noticed through recruitment is how difficult it can be to find good information about the aerospace employment market.

Where are the interesting companies? What should someone with your experience be earning? Is moving to another country financially worthwhile? Does contracting actually pay better?

These are questions I regularly discuss with engineers, so I thought it would be useful to bring the answers together in one place.

I'm not an engineer, and this isn't a website about how to build an aircraft. It's about understanding the industry from a career perspective.

I’ll continue adding to Aerospace Atlas as I learn more about the market. Wherever possible, I'll link to the original sources and make clear where the data has limitations.

Getting in touch

Whether you're actively looking for your next role, curious about the market or simply have something interesting to share, I'm always happy to have a conversation. You don't need to be looking for a job to get in touch.
