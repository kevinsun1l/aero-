European employer directory

Aerospace companies in Europe

A company name tells you surprisingly little about where you would actually work. The same employer might build wings in one country and satellites in another. So I’m collecting locations, not just logos.

Why location matters

If you are thinking about relocating, it helps to know whether there are other employers nearby. If a role does not work out, you may not want to move country all over again. Use the directory to get a feel for the local market, then look at the specific team and vacancies.

This is a growing directory, not a list of live jobs. Activities describe the published site or organisation; they do not prove a specific engineering team is hiring there.
