Contract assumptions

Cost assumptions

Assumes all remaining distributable profit is paid as dividends, no associated companies, no Employment Allowance, no other income, no pension or student loan deductions. Annualised NI; umbrella payroll timing may differ.

Calculation breakdown and limitations

Rates are excluding VAT. Umbrella margin and employer employment costs are removed before gross pay. Holiday pay assumed fully received within the year; no extra holiday deduction. Pension costs are excluded. Limited company losses and retained earnings are not modelled. Confirm with an accountant and your umbrella key information document.
