UK contracting

Understanding IR35

A contract rate is not a salary. Before comparing the two, we need to work out who pays the employment costs, how many weeks you actually bill, and what is left after tax.

Inside and outside IR35

Inside IR35 generally means employment-style tax treatment. An umbrella assignment rate includes costs that are not your gross salary. Outside IR35, a limited company may invoice the client and pay its own costs and taxes. The status depends on the actual engagement, not which option earns more.
