Chapter 02 · Moving across borders

Is a higher salary actually worth moving for?

A salary in another country can look impressive until you account for tax, rent and the currency you spend in. Start with what reaches your bank account, then subtract what it costs you to live.

Two different questions

The national price index gives a broad sense of how expensive a country is. Your own budget tells you something more useful: how much money you might have left at the end of each month. The example budgets below are editable, not verified city averages.
