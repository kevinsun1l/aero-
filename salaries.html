Salaries and job offers

Two people with the same salary might have very different packages. Before looking at a number, I would ask what it includes: pension, bonus, holidays, travel, equity and the actual work you will be doing.

Other parts of a job offer

Ask for the guaranteed base, the conditions for any bonus, the pension contribution and the number of days you are expected in the office. For international offers, compare the monthly net income and actual spending rather than converting gross pay alone.
